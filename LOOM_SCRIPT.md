# Loom Talk Track

## 45-Second Version

Market Bubble Live Desk is a backstage producer feed for running a live Market Bubble show.

Instead of watching Twitch, Kick, and X in separate tabs, the operator gets one source-labeled desk that starts with the useful stuff for the current segment.

The first thing you see is `Next`.

It tells the producer what to use, what it can become, and why it matters. It also gives three simple actions: `Show Best`, open the `Host Queue`, or `Copy Note`.

Under that, the desk keeps the show centered on the three things Market Bubble cares about: make money, leverage AI, and command attention.

The feed is not just raw chat. Each item is labeled by what it can become: an on-air question, a market beat, a segment segue, a clip candidate, or something to park for later.

Each item also gets a pillar label, so the operator knows why it matters: money angle, AI leverage, or attention play.

So the producer is not asking, "What did chat say?"

They are asking, "What should we bring to the hosts next?"

That is the point of the desk: turn messy live audience input into a short, usable show rundown.

## Demo Flow

1. Start at `Next`.
2. Point out that the default feed already shows the best items for this show part.
3. Click an `Angle` chip to narrow by show strategy.
4. Point out the `Potential` and angle labels on feed rows.
5. Click `Topics` to show what people are mentioning.
6. Click `Host Queue` to show the operator queue.
7. End on `Copy Note`: this is the clean handoff for the hosts.

## Closing Line

For Market Bubble, this is not a generic chat aggregator. It is an internal live desk that helps the operator decide what is worth saying, saving, clipping, or parking while the show is happening.
